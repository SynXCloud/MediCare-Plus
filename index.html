Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment? +

Simply click "Book Appointment," choose your department or doctor, select a date and time, and fill in your name and phone number. No registration or login required. You'll receive a confirmation SMS.

What are the OPD timings? +

Morning OPD: 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM. Evening OPD: 5:00 PM – 8:00 PM. The Emergency department is open 24 hours, 7 days a week, including public holidays.

Do you accept insurance? +

Yes, we offer cashless treatment with 200+ insurance providers including Star Health, HDFC ERGO, Bajaj Allianz, ICICI Lombard, and all government health schemes. Please carry your insurance card at the time of visit.

How do I access my reports digitally? +

Lab reports are typically ready within 2 hours and sent via SMS link to your registered mobile number. You can also access them through our Prescription Portal by entering your patient ID and mobile number.

Is parking available? +

Yes, we have a multi-level parking facility with 300+ spaces available for patients and visitors. The first 2 hours are complimentary for patients with an active OPD token.

Can I reschedule or cancel an appointment? +

Yes, appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Use the link in your confirmation SMS or call our helpline at 1800-000-999.
